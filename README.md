![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/eeH7jU8.png "Pokémon Pink Version")![alt text]


This project is what I imagined a hypothetical ‘Pokémon Pink’ would look like when rumors of one were mentioned in the Giga Leak™. Along with the trivia of Clefairy once being the potential mascot for the series before Pikachu, and the often overlooked Pocket Monsters manga where Red chooses Clefairy as his starter; there is pleanty of good reference material. With that said, it was thanks to seeing Sindorman's animation on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/B_dAOwBJTfq/), then around the same time Artsy-Theo posted these four emotion portriats on [Tumblr](https://www.tumblr.com/artsy-theo/180634822998/pokemon-pinkspecial-clefairy-edition-clefairy?source=share), and everything afterwards has just been me trying my best, having never coded in assembly before, though I have gotten some assistance on pret's Discord from the lovely people over there.

(Did YOU Know? Pokémon Green had its own exclusive Super GameBoy palette? The international releases of Red & Blue were built off of Jp Blue, which itself was built off of Jp Red; then the devs forgot about this detail, and Pokemon Red's palette was used twice. Every town across Kanto had their colors slightly shifted from between Red and Green, but most notible was the colors used for Pallet Town (and Lorelei room). Cinnabar Island would have used the same colors, except the background shade of white had been tinted in each versions respective color.

![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/ffRKY5P.png "Pokémon Yellow SBG")![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/yMbw4du.png "Pokémon Green SBG")![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/lTd9577.png "Pokémon Yellow GBC")

Point being, Pokemon Yellow was built off Green, and used it's palette as a base for its own. You can see the shared purple hue in Pallet town, but what also stands out is the drastic change in brightness and satursation between the colors used for Yellow. I believe in their attempt to tint the game in yellow, they decided to really crank up the exposure on the Super GameBoy palette. On the other hand, they likely needed to overly saturate the colors for the GameBoy Color just to be noticable on their non-backlit display.

![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/2AcJYff.png "Pokémon Pink SBG")![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/SQfI6em.png "Pokémon Pink SBG")![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/Qj3r7ne.png "Pokémon Pink GBC")

All palettes across the game have been modified to heavily reference Pokemon Red's colors whenever possible. I also restored the old cave palette, while retaining Yellow's cave colors for Cerulean Cave (arguably the old palette would fit better for Cerulean Cave since it is more bluish, but whatever). I also have reverted the drastic changes to the Generic and Grey palettes that affected Pokemon like Mew and Rattata (Gamefreak borrowed the Generic palette for Pikachu's Title Screen colors for some reason, which is used for party sprites, trainer cards, and much more...). Though since some Pokemon benefited from the change like Geodude and Jynx, I have seperated these into their own palette lines so we can have the best of both. Note that some sprites have been edited to better take use of their colors (Rattata, Tauros); while unrelated, some back sprites have been edited to better represent their official designs, whiele retaining their Gen 1 stylings.

Generic trainers that were edited for Yellow to reflect specific Pokemon anime episodes have been reverted to their Red & Blue encounters. (Though Jessie and James are still present, having integrated themselves much more heavily in each map's scripting. I just thought it best to leave them for the time being, but who knows? Maybe in the future I might attempt something...) Meanwhile unlike Red, Blue, or Yellow, I have chose to forgo any attempt at Rival starter balancing, and decided instead to stick close to his Pocket Monsters manga counterpart, even slightly breaking establighed patterns in rematch fights and allowing your Rival slightly wider access to his caught team. Rival and Gym Leader encounters asd well have been edited to reflect their Pocket Monster manga teams. One thing notiable about Pokemon Yellow was it's removal of all Pikachu across the region, including from trainer teams, with the only exception being Lt. Surge's Raichu. To reflect this, all Clefairy have been removed, removed Pikachu have been restored, and Elite Four Agatha's team has been edited to contain the only Clefable in the region.

Wild Pokemon with regonized version pairings have had their encounters flipped, while version exclusive Pokemon have been swapped with their respective counterparts. Here is a general overview.

The thing I am most proud of (besides the Title Screen animation. I had no part in the art, but learning how to code it was a fun nightmare) is the new events added to refernce the Pocket Monster's manga. Trainers Blue and Yellow have been added at points that roughly fit with their manga story progression, but their interactions have been sanitized and altered to more fit with the events of the game, as the manga really goes off with its crude humor. Their name have also been reduced to a nickname, as I didn't feel it necessary to name them, while keeping their titles of Blue and Yellow felt odd when the player could always name their Rival Blue as well. Busybody can be rematched in his Trashed House after every encounter with him, and Imposter acts as the post-game fight. Yellow in the Manga only ever had Pikachu, so I had the rest of his team reflect Pocket Monsters manga Red.

Other things I've changed are the Slot Machine graphics to fit the Clefairy aesthetic. I let Prof. Oak heal you when registering your team in the Hall of Fame after always forgetting to heal afterwards. Clefairy can now learn Surf (also TM Swift) and can visit the Surfing house. The S.S. Anne now will redock after beating the champion. (No new trainers or parties, this is just for those who might have missed an item or wanted to read the restored harbor sign). Two new cutable trees have been added to ease backtracking without breaking the flow of progression. Because plot related reasons, Mewtwo has been replaced with the Virtual Console Event Mew, and will have the proper OT and ID number when caught.
